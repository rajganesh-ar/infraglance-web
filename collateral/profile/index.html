The problem

Sites are full of cameras.
Very few are watched.

Most plants already record every shift. The footage is reviewed after an incident, not during it, while inspections, rounds and surveys still depend on someone being in the right place at the right moment.

01

Recorded, not watched

CCTV is searched after the event. Dashboards report after the fact, so warnings reach people in tomorrow’s report instead of at the point of work.

02

Detectors without context

A helmet in frame is not a helmet worn. One rule applied everywhere ignores the zone, the task and the permit, and one violation fires dozens of frame-level alerts.

03

Gaps in manual inspection

Manual inspection is slow, risky and inconsistent between inspectors. Colour checks drift with factory lighting, and findings sit in PDFs that cannot be compared year to year.

04

Blind spots in the plant

Micro-stops under two minutes are never logged. Brownfield plants have hundreds of analog gauges and no sensors, and every conveyor failure mode needs its own point sensor.

What changes

Infraglance puts the cameras you already have to work. AI on site understands the rule for each zone, task and permit, measures what it sees and turns it into one event with evidence, in time for someone to act.

Pillar 01 · Industrial safety

Glance Guard

Safety that understands context, not just objects.

Guard turns existing CCTV into a safety system. It checks whether the rule for this zone, this task and this permit is actually being followed, then warns people at the point of work, not in tomorrow’s report.

Worn, not just present

Each PPE item is mapped to the right body point: helmet on head, goggles over eyes, mask over nose.

Rules by zone, task and permit

A PPE matrix of zone, shift, task and permit, so hot work and confined spaces get the right checks.

One violation, one event

De-duplicated across frames and cameras, with duration and severity, and escalated only if it persists.

Warnings at the point of work

Speakers, strobes and LED signs announce the rule without naming anyone.

Pillar 02 · Product quality

Glance Inspect

See every defect, and every shade, before it ships.

Inspect learns from good parts only, measures colour difference against your master sample under calibrated light, and turns anomaly scores into pass, review or reject decisions your line can act on.

Trained on good parts

50–200 good images per SKU, no code, and recipes that make changeovers a matter of minutes.

Colour measured, not guessed

D65 lighting, per-shift reference-card calibration and ΔE2000 against your master sample.

Decisions, not heatmaps

Cost-weighted pass, review or reject per SKU, with the nearest good example shown as evidence.

Quality data for the plant

Defect spikes linked to machine parameters, shift, batch and supplier lot.

Pillar 03 · Plant condition

Glance Sense

Your plant’s baseline, and every deviation from it.

Sense learns what normal looks like for every camera, by shift and time of day, and flags leaks, vapour, spills, blocked walkways and hot spots. It also reads the analog gauges your operators still walk past on rounds.

A baseline per camera

Scene-level anomaly baselines, aware of shift and time of day, refreshed when conditions drift.

Analog gauges, digitised

Dial gauges, sight glasses and meters read from existing cameras, with trends and limit alarms.

Thermal hot spots

Panels, motors, bearings and transformers watched against limits and rate of rise.

Video plus PLC

Valve closed and vapour visible is a leak; motor on and nothing moving is a jam.

Pillar 04 · Conveyors & line flow

Glance Flow

One camera watching what used to need ten sensors.

Flow monitors bulk-material conveyors for rips, drift, spillage, blockages and hot idlers, and production lines for jams, gaps, fallen products and the micro-stops nobody logs.

Many failure modes, one unit

Rip, drift, spillage, blockage and foreign objects covered by one rugged vision unit.

Hot idlers found early

A thermal camera covering a run of idlers flags overheating before a bearing fails.

Micro-stops counted

Automatic stop detection feeds a true OEE picture by station and shift.

Fused with PLC signals

Motor on and no movement means a jam, not an empty belt.

Our AI ethics charter

01

Safety, not surveillance

We do not build tools to monitor individual productivity.

02

No individual performance scoring

Analytics report by zone, station and shift, never as rankings of people.

03

No facial recognition by default

Identification only where contractually required, lawful and disclosed to workers.

04

No emotion recognition

We do not infer emotions, sentiment or personality from video.

05

A human can always override

Every alert can be reviewed, dismissed or escalated by a person.

06

Transparency with the workforce

Each deployment documents what is and is not detected, with notice templates.

07

Measured, not claimed

Accuracy measured on your own data and reported monthly, false alarms included.

08

Regulation-ready

Designed for GDPR, India’s DPDP Act and the EU AI Act: logging, oversight, minimisation.
