The problem

Cameras everywhere. Nobody watching.

Most sites already have the cameras. What they lack is something watching them, on every shift.

  1. 01

    Recorded, not watched

    CCTV runs on every shift, yet footage is opened only after something has gone wrong.

  2. 02

    Checked by hand

    Parts, gauges and structures are inspected by sampling and rounds: slow, risky at height and inconsistent between inspectors.

  3. 03

    Reported after the fact

    Dashboards count yesterday’s violations, while the hazard is happening now, at the point of work.

02 · Glance Inspect · Product quality

See every defect, and every shade, before it ships.

Inspect learns from good parts only, measures colour difference against your master sample under calibrated light, and turns anomaly scores into pass, review or reject decisions for the line.

What it sees
  • Surface defects, trained on good parts
  • Colour and shade difference (ΔE2000)
  • Missing, wrong or flipped components
  • Labels, codes, caps and seals

Your plant’s baseline, and every deviation from it.
