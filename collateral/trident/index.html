The pressures

Four pressures on a closures plant.

01

Zero-defect closures

Bottlers expect every cap to seal, every tamper band to hold and every lot to match the brand colour.

Seal · tamper band · colour
02

Power cuts and restarts

The 2024 drought brought load-shedding of 8–12+ hours a day. Every restart costs warm-up time and start-up scrap.

2024 · 8–12+ h a day
03

Imported resin, moving currency

Resin is imported, and the kwacha hit a record low of about K28 to the US dollar in January 2025, so every scrapped cap costs more.

Jan 2025 · about K28/US$
04

New duties

Since 30 December 2025, the OHS Act No. 16 of 2025 requires employers with 10 or more staff to run a safety committee and an approved risk assessment every two years, with daily penalties. EPR regulations cover plastic packaging.

OHS Act 2025 · EPR

Sources: News24 (2024) on load-shedding; TimesLIVE and Bloomberg (2025) on the kwacha; OHS Act No. 16 of 2025; Extended Producer Responsibility Regulations 2018.
